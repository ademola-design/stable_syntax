01 — Services

Your website is the job. Everything else supports it.

Whether you need five pages or five hundred, the work is the same — there is just more of it. Everything here either builds your website or keeps it working once it is live.

Building your website

The main thing we do, at whatever size your business needs it. We build your site to fit how you actually work, rather than forcing it into a ready-made template designed for someone else. It does what you need, it opens quickly, and it can grow as you grow — so you are not paying to start again in two years.

  • Small businesses
  • Growing companies
  • Large organisations
  • Online shops

How it looks and works

We decide what each page shows and where everything sits, so a visitor finds what they came for without having to think about it.

Works on every phone

Most people will visit your site on a phone, not a computer. We check yours on both, and on tablets in between, so you never lose a customer to a page that will not fit their screen.

Pages that open fast

People give up on a slow website, and search engines push it further down the results. We work out what is actually slowing yours down and fix that, instead of guessing.

Getting found on Google

We set your site up so search engines can tell what your business does and show it to people looking for it. What we will not do is promise you the top spot — nobody can honestly promise that.

Your look, kept the same everywhere

Your logo, your colours, and simple rules for using them. So your website, your invoices and your social posts all look like they came from the same business — whether we made them or you did.

  • Logo
  • Colours and fonts
  • A simple brand guide
  • Cards and flyers

03 — Process

The same four steps, whatever the size.

A one-page site and a large build run through exactly this. What changes is how long each step takes, and we tell you that up front rather than at the end.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    A conversation about the business, what the site has to do for it, and who it is for. No charge, and no obligation to carry on.

  2. 02

    We plan and design

    A plan scoped to your size, and a design you see before anyone writes code. You know what you are getting before we build it.

  3. 03

    We build

    Clean, current code, with regular updates as it comes together — so the finished site is never the first version you have seen.

  4. 04

    Launch and support

    We put it live, check it works where your customers are, and stay reachable afterwards. Launch day is not the handover.
